## CRITICAL NOTE ON BOT CREATION

The API **DOES NOT** expose separate endpoints for:
- CreateGreetingBotModel
- CreateTaggingBotModel  
- CreateSectorForwardingBotModel

These are RESPONSE models only. The **ONLY** bot creation endpoint is:
- **POST /v1/bots/flowchart/** - Creates flowchart bots

The greeting, tagging, and sector forwarding bots are created through the 
flowchart bot system, not as separate endpoints.
